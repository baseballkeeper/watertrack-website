Delete your WaterTrack data

Steps for removing hydration records from your device and contacting MPM Ventures LLC about data deletion requests.

WaterTrack does not require an account. Hydration history is stored on your device. You can delete individual entries in the app or remove all local app storage using Android settings.

Delete individual hydration entries

Open WaterTrack, go to Today or History, find the entry you want to remove, and tap Delete. This removes the selected entry from WaterTrack's local history.

Delete all locally stored WaterTrack data

On your Android device:

  1. Open your device's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and select WaterTrack.
  3. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage or Clear data and confirm.

This removes locally stored hydration history and WaterTrack preferences, including goals, quick-add settings, and reminder configuration. Menu labels vary by Android device. Clearing app storage is irreversible unless you have a backup. You may also uninstall the app, but device or cloud backups may remain.

Request help with deletion of data collected outside your device

If you enabled optional usage analytics, or have a question about data we may control, email watertrackbusiness@gmail.com with the subject WaterTrack Data Deletion Request. Tell us whether your request concerns analytics or another issue. You do not need a WaterTrack account. Please do not email your hydration logs or other sensitive information.

We will review your request, explain what information we can identify and delete, and provide any applicable retention details. WaterTrack does not have user accounts or a server-side copy of your hydration history, so we cannot remotely clear your device's local records. Firebase Analytics data may not be identifiable from an email address alone.

What is deleted or retained?

  • Deleted when you clear WaterTrack app storage: locally stored hydration entries, goals, presets, reminder preferences, and other locally stored app settings.
  • Not automatically deleted: JSON backups or text exports you saved elsewhere, copies in Android/device backups, and hydration records previously written to Health Connect. Delete these separately using the storage provider's or Health Connect's controls.
  • Optional Firebase Analytics: turning off analytics stops future collection on that installation but does not automatically delete records previously received by Google. Analytics retention depends on the configured Google Analytics property and applicable Google policies. We review requests for any records we can identify and control; we cannot guarantee that an email address alone can be matched to an anonymous app instance.
  • Deletion-request correspondence: emails you send us may be retained as needed to respond to and document the request, subject to applicable legal obligations.
